This module extends the sales order management to include credit card
fees based on the installments defined by the Sale Invoice Plan.

It allows you to:

- Mark a payment method as **Card Administrator** and configure its fee
  table per installment range directly in the payment method form
  (**Fee Ranges** tab).
- Define, on the sale order, the fee configuration for each selected
  payment method marked as Card Administrator: **Add Fee**,
  **Credit Card Fee Range**, the **Amount** the fee is charged on and the
  resulting **Fee Amount**.
- Optionally sum the credit card fee to the sale order total.
- Include the credit card fee in each installment invoice,
  proportionally to the planned amount.
- Show the credit card fee of the sale order in the customer invoice form,
  both as a listing of the fees per card administrator and in the totals of
  the invoice.
- View the fee distribution across installments in the invoice plan.
